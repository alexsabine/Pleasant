# Relational weave, holding and possibility space

This is a conceptual bridge, not core mathematics.

The revised CRR reading of attachment/Winnicott should not use a universal `Omega` openness dial.

Instead separate:
- intrinsic historical trajectory `C`;
- endpoint/chord `C*`;
- surplus `S`;
- historical resources used in regeneration;
- the presently represented viable continuation set;
- return/recovery cost under relational support.

A secure relationship can be modelled heuristically as lowering the cost of returning to viable organisation, thereby enlarging the safely explorable set. This preserves the intuition:

> **Holding does not make another system open. It makes openness safer.**

Winnicott's unresolved created/found paradox belongs in `CONCORDANCE` unless a specific empirical bridge is frozen.
