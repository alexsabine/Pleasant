# Pilot-safe prospective research plan

## Baseline first
No research model should be required for Kim's 8-week acceptance. Collect only consented/appropriate human feedback and editorial corrections. No children's identity/travel data for ML training.

## Study A: freshness and review prioritisation
- Input: verified listing revision/validation histories and synthetic missing/duplicate data.
- Baseline: fixed review intervals by category.
- Challenger 1: calibrated Bayesian evidence-aware review priority (EVSI).
- Challenger 2: same Bayesian priority plus predeclared Fisher–Rao belief-change feature/triggers.
- Outcomes: fraction of outdated entries discovered before user harm, verification effort, false flags, calibration (Brier/log loss) and accessibility errors.
- Controls: copy of stale observation counted once; a no-change long elapsed interval; actual changed record but near-identical text.
- Result claiming: challenger 2 must beat challenger 1 on held-out data; otherwise novelty/CRR-specific gain not established.

## Study B: information dosage
- Users specify few/standard/all. No covert user-trait inference.
- Baselines: 3-item answer, deterministic relevant shortlist, ordinary utility hysteresis.
- Challenger: independently preselected Fisher–Rao belief-change controller with identical output budget.
- Outcomes: verified-answer suitability, number of corrective turns, felt control, opted-in satisfaction, unnecessary question rate.
- Include abstention and explicit 'show all' escape hatch.

## Study C: cross-profession governance
- Randomized matched briefing for volunteer editors, caregivers and developers: same neutral systems facts with / without a CRR-inspired process lexicon.
- Blinded rubric: identify stale evidence, unauthorized representation, undesired feedback loops, clear review decision. Include negative cases where the metaphor misleads.

## Register before test
Freeze data split, policies, thresholds, priors, loss weights, tolerance margins, computational budget, sampling, exclusions and outcome aggregation. Reviewer distinguishes a genuine result from a standard Bayesian reduction or a helpful explanatory grammar.
