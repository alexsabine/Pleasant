4. What stays constant across any palette

Roads are the lightest line on the map, water is the only blue, parks are the only green, and the brand accent is reserved for the user's own selections and pins. Labels never drop below 14 px on phones. Every panel carries its credit strip and vintage. Hazard layers carry their disclaimer on the map itself, not in a tooltip.
