# Ethical operating code / representational ethics

**Maps change territory:** publishing a place and recommending it changes public attention, visits and sometimes people's reputations and local ecology. Record where the atlas's own recommendations have contributed to public actions where observable/consented; do not assume all changes are its doing.

1. **A person isn't a pin, score or profile.** No covert profiling; caregivers choose topics and age bands without identifying children.
2. **Ask before connecting people.** Contacts, introductions, messaging, planned itineraries and GPS sharing require proper authority and consent. Never secretly impersonate a person in WhatsApp.
3. **Verified, reported, inferred and generated are not interchangeable.** Publish source, last-verified date, uncertainty and right of correction.
4. **Protect the vulnerable.** Avoid public homes of children, sensitive wildlife, confidential health/safety cases, and unpublished cultural sites.
5. **Leave room for difference.** Preserve multiple source observations and disagreement; never flatten into a single generated consensus. Moderation may protect people while still allowing criticism and whistleblowing.
6. **Quiet is a valid outcome.** Allow no recommendation, no notification and no introduction. Do not optimise for clicks, message volume, emotional intensity or reported synchronicity.
7. **People govern.** Monthly review of consented feedback, audit logs, corrections, decisions, appeals and unintended effects; urgent safeguarding escalates immediately.
8. **Local stakeholders shape representation.** Consult local family services, disability groups, community members and appropriate Tribal stakeholders before publishing culturally sensitive layers or labels.
9. **Permissions are specific.** A trusted contributor may maintain their verified programme without access to private journeys or other people's profiles.
10. **Correct, export and delete where appropriate.** Apply defined retention periods and deletion rights; maintain necessary audit history only under a justified policy.

## Monthly governance loop
Observation / feedback -> categorised review -> director decision -> named owner / due date -> change -> evaluation -> plain-language report. Report both successes and unintended exclusions.

## Family and child boundary
Default product is **caregiver-directed**, not child account tracking. No publicly visible home-to-school routes, live tracking of children or automatic inferred family relationships. Generalised safety and accessibility fields only when source checked. Hazard maps carry source and date and never substitute for emergency officials.
