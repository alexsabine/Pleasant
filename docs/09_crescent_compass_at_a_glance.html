Crescent Compass · for Kim · October 2026

Crescent Compass at a glance

Eleven simple T-charts covering the whole process, from a visitor opening the map to a steward approving a new place. Lumen is the site's assistant.

1

Who can do what

Anyone, no account

  • See the whole map
  • Read every listing and its source
  • See this week's events
  • Find local help

Members, signed in

  • Ask Lumen
  • Add a place or photo
  • Send a correction
  • Save favourite places
2

Joining

Member does

  1. Opens the map
  2. Taps Join
  3. Confirms they are an adult
  4. Picks age bands and budget, or skips

Crescent Compass does

  1. Shows everything, no sign-up
  2. Creates the account
  3. Asks nothing about children
  4. Shapes Lumen's answers with those settings
3

Asking Lumen

Member sees

  1. Types any question
  2. Answer appears word by word
  3. Cards with map, save, directions
  4. Source and check date on each place

Behind the scenes

  1. Lumen reads it (Haiku)
  2. Searches published listings only
  3. The 75% rule picks what to recommend
  4. Each sentence checked, then sealed in the log
4

Adding a place or photo

Member does

  1. Taps Add, or tells Lumen
  2. Adds a photo and a note
  3. Taps the spot on the map
  4. Gets a note when it's live

Crescent Compass does

  1. Removes hidden GPS data
  2. Checks for a duplicate nearby
  3. Pre-checks the photo overnight (Opus)
  4. Holds it in Pending for a steward
5

A steward's month

Lumen prepares

  • A list of everything pending
  • Evidence for each item
  • A suggested action
  • Websites that changed

Steward decides

  • Asks "What needs approving?"
  • Taps Approve, Reject or Ask
  • About an hour a month
  • Each decision sealed in the log
6

Lumen's limits

Lumen can

  • Answer from published listings
  • Suggest, explain and draft
  • Say "I don't know"
  • Remember the current chat

Lumen can never

  • Publish or approve
  • Invent a place or fact
  • See accounts or private notes
  • Give anyone a role
7

The two AI models

Haiku, the front desk

  • Every chat turn
  • Any phrasing, any language
  • Fast and cheap
  • About a quarter of a cent a turn

Opus, the specialist

  • Photo checks
  • Website comparisons
  • The steward's digest
  • Hard questions, about 1 in 20
8

With and without AI

Works with no AI, free

  • Map, filters, listings
  • The 75% rule and the sealed log
  • Spotting changed websites
  • Events calendar, approvals

Needs AI, paid

  • Understanding any question
  • Natural, friendly wording
  • Judging photos
  • Explaining what changed

10

The Quadruplicate

We use

  • Its community calendar feed
  • Its list of local calendars
  • Its directory, to double-check ours
  • Links to safety alerts and meetings

We agree first

  • Credit on every item we show
  • Share-alike terms for its data
  • Which events are its to share
  • What we send back
11

Active Inference Institute tools: GEO_INFER and GNN

Worth adding, with Daniel

  • A safety banner from official alert feeds (weather, earthquake, tsunami) that says plainly when a feed is down
  • Lumen's decision maths written in GNN notation, so stewards and Kim can read the models
  • Links to relevant city code sections, once rights are agreed
  • A guard that keeps map layers small and fast
  • A research comparison of our freshness model with theirs, offline only

Leave out

  • Running either toolkit on our server: large, fast-changing, still in beta
  • Property-owner, mortgage and tribal-territory data
  • Tracking where people are, or scoring people
  • Our own evacuation routes: link the official ones
  • Copying their code: it is licensed for non-commercial use only
