What Crescent Compass is

Crescent Compass is a community map and noticeboard, with a guide. It helps people find places and helps groups arrange to meet, but it does not run, staff or supervise any activity. Whoever runs an activity is responsible for it, and parents stay responsible for their children. The terms of use say this in one plain sentence, so everyone knows where responsibility sits.

  • Public events come from organisers' own websites and calendars, are checked by a steward, and link back to their source.
  • Group meetups are arranged by members, always at public places. Only a count of who is coming is shown, and reminders go by email to those who ask. No text messages.
  • Photos show places, not people: no identifiable children, location data removed, and a steward approves each one. (Registering a US copyright agent, $6 every three years, is optional.)
  • Children: accounts are for adults aged 18 and over, and nothing is collected about children.
  • Members' data: anyone can download or delete their data at any time from My atlas.

This is lawful because people are told and agree: everyone through the Privacy page and Lumen's panel, and stewards through a signed agreement. When the AI is switched on, Lumen's panel also says questions go to Anthropic.

One rule, unchanged. A listing is recommended while the chance it is right is above 75 percent. Steward trust feeds into that chance and adds no new rule.
Trust sets the re-check date. Reliable entries are recommended for longer before a re-check; newer or less certain ones are re-checked sooner. Nobody is blocked from contributing.
Fair blame. Only an entry that was wrong when made counts against a steward, never one that changed later. Lumen's own mistakes count against Lumen.
Proved first. Everyone is treated equally until real re-check results show personal trust predicts better. The maths is in research/13_steward_trust.md (7 of 7 checks pass).

Becoming a steward

  1. Track record: at least 20 approved items over six months, then a year as an editor.
  2. Invited by at least three of the four founding stewards. No number does this on its own.
  3. Signs the steward agreement online, by typing their name. The founding four sign it too.
  4. Mentored for three months, then full steward access, checked by the server on every action.

Before members can join

  1. Approve the Privacy page and add a contact email.
  2. Publish short terms of use: what Crescent Compass is, 18 and over, photo rules, and how to ask for something to be removed.
  3. Secure sign-in, with two-step sign-in for stewards, and a one-page breach plan.
  4. Accept each service's standard data terms when the accounts are set up.

Legal summaries reviewed

  • US privacy thresholds: Civil Code 1798.140, California Privacy Protection Agency
  • Privacy notices: Business and Professions Code 22575–22577 (the owner is responsible, not whoever hosts the site); FTC
  • Security and breach notice: Civil Code 1798.82, California Attorney General
  • Children: FTC COPPA FAQs
  • AI and recording: bot disclosure law; website chats and California's wiretap law: Manatt, Covington; Anthropic Usage Policy, Anthropic data terms
  • Events, texts and email: FCC, FTC CAN-SPAM guide; youth organisations: Business and Professions Code 18975 (applies to groups that supervise children, not to a map)
  • Photos and licences: US Copyright Office, Digital Media Law Project
  • Domain: CIRA
