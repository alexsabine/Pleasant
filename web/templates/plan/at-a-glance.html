1

Who can do what

Anyone, no account

  • See the whole map
  • Read every listing and its source
  • See this week's events
  • Find local help

Members, signed in

  • Ask Lumen
  • Add a place or photo
  • Send a correction
  • Save favourite places
2

Joining

Member does

  1. Opens the map
  2. Taps Join
  3. Confirms they are an adult
  4. Picks age bands and budget, or skips

Crescent Compass does

  1. Shows everything, no sign-up
  2. Creates the account
  3. Asks nothing about children
  4. Shapes Lumen's answers with those settings
3

Asking Lumen

Member sees

  1. Types any question
  2. Answer appears word by word
  3. Cards with map, save, directions
  4. Source and check date on each place

Behind the scenes

  1. Lumen reads it (Haiku)
  2. Searches published listings only
  3. The 75% rule picks what to recommend
  4. Each sentence checked, then sealed in the log
4

Adding a place or photo

Member does

  1. Taps Add, or tells Lumen
  2. Adds a photo and a note
  3. Taps the spot on the map
  4. Gets a note when it's live

Crescent Compass does

  1. Removes hidden GPS data
  2. Checks for a duplicate nearby
  3. Pre-checks the photo overnight (Opus)
  4. Holds it in Pending for a steward
5

A steward's month

Lumen prepares

  • A list of everything pending
  • Evidence for each item
  • A suggested action
  • Websites that changed

Steward decides

  • Asks "What needs approving?"
  • Taps Approve, Reject or Ask
  • Or updates the map any time via Lumen
  • Each decision sealed in the log
6

Lumen's limits

Lumen can

  • Answer from published listings
  • Suggest, explain and draft
  • Say "I don't know"
  • Remember the chat for six hours

Lumen can never

  • Publish or approve
  • Invent a place or fact
  • See accounts or private notes
  • Give anyone a role
7

The two AI models

Haiku, the front desk

  • Every chat turn
  • Any phrasing, any language
  • Fast and cheap
  • About a quarter of a cent a turn

Opus, the specialist

  • Photo checks
  • Website comparisons
  • The steward's digest
  • Hard questions, about 1 in 20
8

With and without AI

Works with no AI, free

  • Map, filters, listings
  • The 75% rule and the sealed log
  • Spotting changed websites
  • Events calendar, approvals

Needs AI, paid

  • Understanding any question
  • Natural, friendly wording
  • Judging photos
  • Explaining what changed

Daniel Friedman's tools: GEO_INFER and GNN

GEO_INFER ideas we can use

  • A safety banner from official weather, earthquake and tsunami feeds that says plainly when a feed is down
  • Crescent City tide times from NOAA's harbour station
  • A rights-and-source record for every map layer
  • A community representation check to guide who is invited to become a steward
  • Strict time stamps on every feed, so old data never looks new

GNN, for the maths

  • Lumen's freshness model is now written in GNN notation
  • The translation was checked both ways and matches exactly
  • It gives Kim and stewards a readable record of the model
  • It serves as an offline research comparison
  • Live decisions stay with the 75% rule

Both are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, so we use the ideas in our own code or agree terms with Daniel. Under our privacy rules we leave out property-owner and tribal data and any profiling of people.

12

Onboarding and stewards

Founding stewards

  • Kim, Sasha, Daniel and Bill
  • Add or amend the map at any time, through Lumen
  • Run the monthly round for general updates
  • Invite new stewards and agree system changes

How a new steward joins

  1. Joins as a member and starts contributing
  2. Builds a reliable record, then becomes an editor
  3. Is invited by at least three of the four founding stewards
  4. Signs the steward agreement, spends three months alongside a mentor, and is recorded in the log

Later, once there are more stewards, an invitation needs a majority of all stewards, with care for which communities are under-represented.

13

Coordinating a group event

The group does

  1. An organiser asks Lumen: "Set up a tide-pool walk for our playgroup on Saturday"
  2. Picks a public meeting place from the map
  3. Members tap "I'm coming"
  4. Everyone adds it to their phone calendar, with directions

Lumen does

  1. Checks the meeting place is public and verified, never a home
  2. Suggests a time using official tide times and alerts
  3. Sends reminders only to people who asked for them
  4. Tells everyone if the time or place changes, then archives the event afterwards

Figures are estimates from October 2026, using Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 and $0.50 and Claude Opus 5.5 at $4 and $20 per million tokens in and out. The Quadruplicate's code and data are CC BY-SA 4.0; GEO_INFER and GNN are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
