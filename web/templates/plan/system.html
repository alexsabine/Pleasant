The four journeys through the system

JourneyPathAI used
BrowsingVisitor, then the public map on GitHub Pages. No account, no server, no tracking.None
Asking LumenMember, then member pages, then Lumen, which reads published listings, checks its answer and records it in the log.Haiku for most turns; Opus for about 1 in 20
ContributingMember, then permissions, then Pending, then the overnight pre-check, then the steward's monthly round.Opus, overnight, at half price
ApprovingSteward, then the stewards' desk, then permissions, then Published and the sealed log. The public copy refreshes that night.Opus writes the digest; a person decides

What never leaves our server

  • Accounts, email addresses, saved places and members' settings.
  • Unpublished items in Pending, and steward notes.
  • Original photos, and any GPS data, which is removed on arrival.
  • The decision rules: what to recommend, who may do what, and what is published.

Claude receives only a member's question, the published listings that might answer it, and photos sent for checking. Anthropic's commercial terms say API data is not used to train its models by default; confirm this when the account is opened. Nothing is sent at all when the AI is switched off.
