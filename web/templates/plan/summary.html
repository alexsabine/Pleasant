What each part does

PartWhat it does, in plain words
Public map and listingsShows verified places on a map and as a list, with filters for age, cost, access and community. Each listing shows its source and when it was last checked.
LumenThe guide for members. It reads a question, looks up published listings, decides what it can recommend, writes a short answer and shows its sources. It cannot publish anything.
JoiningAn optional account with an adult confirmation. Members choose age bands, budget and access needs, or skip. Nothing is asked about children.
PendingA waiting room for everything new: places, photos, corrections, changed websites and calendar events. Nothing in Pending is public.
Stewards' monthly roundA steward asks Lumen "what needs approving this month?", reads one line per item with its evidence and a suggestion, and taps Approve, Reject or Ask for more.
Evidence and the sealed logEvery published fact is tied to a checked source. Every approval, change and Lumen answer goes into a log that shows any later tampering.
FreshnessEach listing's confidence fades as time passes since its last check. Lumen recommends a place only while it is more than 75 percent likely to be current.
Review queueTells stewards which listings most need a re-check, so a few checks a week keep the whole map trustworthy.
Monthly checksThe server re-reads each listing's official website. Pages that changed go into Pending, with the old and new text side by side.
PhotosHidden GPS data is removed on arrival. The member confirms the place by hand. A photo goes public only after a steward approves it.
The QuadruplicateDaniel Friedman's civic snapshot of Crescent City. Its community calendar feeds Pending, and its safety and meeting pages are linked.
Map filesOur own copy of the map from public data. Browsing sends nothing to Google or other map companies.
PermissionsThe server checks who is allowed to do what on every action. No AI can change that.

How an update reaches the map

  1. Something new arrives. A member adds a place or photo or sends a correction. A website changes, or a calendar event appears.
  2. It is tidied and held. GPS data is stripped, duplicates are flagged, and the item waits in Pending.
  3. It is pre-checked overnight. Lumen's specialist model looks at photos, compares changed pages and drafts a one-line summary with a suggested action.
  4. A steward decides. At the monthly round, or sooner, a steward approves, rejects or asks the member for more.
  5. It goes live and is sealed. The map updates, the listing's freshness clock starts, and the decision is written to the log.
  6. It is watched. As time passes the listing's confidence fades, and the review queue brings it back for a re-check before Lumen stops recommending it.

From member to steward

Responsibility grows with a track record, never with popularity, and only by invitation. The figures decide who may be invited; stewards invite, the member accepts, and the server records it.

StepCan doMay be invited when
MemberAsk Lumen, add places and photos, send correctionsJoins with an adult confirmation
ContributorDraft listings straight into the review queueAt least 5 approved items, with a reliable track record
EditorCheck evidence and mark it verified; cannot publishAt least 20 approved items over 6 months, across at least 3 kinds of place
StewardApprove publication, run the monthly round, propose rule changesAn editor for a year, invited by the stewards, with care for which communities are under-represented

"Reliable" uses a cautious estimate of how often a person's contributions are approved: 20 approvals and no rejections scores 0.87; with 2 rejections, 0.75. Old results fade over a year. Details are in the repository, in research/11_stewardship_pathway.md.
