About this atlas

Pleasant Crescent is a caregiver-directed directory for Del Norte County. Every listing shows its source and the date it was last verified. Unknown is shown as unknown. Nothing is tracked about children.

How visibility works

A contributor suggests; an editor verifies the claim against a source; an administrator approves publication. Each step is written to a hash-chained audit log. Crescent Compass reads only the published projection and records the provenance of every answer.

Correct a listing

Use the correction form on any listing page, or email the editors. Corrections become claims that an editor checks before anything changes publicly.

Governance

The freshness model, decision constants and the Compass policy are published in the repository (research/04_mathematics_register.md) and editable by administrators without code changes. Monthly review covers corrections, audit-log entries and Compass abstentions.
