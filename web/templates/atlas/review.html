Questions for Kim

· a first draft of Crescent Compass, built to be argued with ·

This is a working draft, not a launch. Every place on it was summarised from an official website and placed from an authoritative source, then checked against the county's own boundaries and elevation data. None has been visited yet. The point of this draft is to make the decisions below concrete enough to answer.

What to look at

01

Explore the nine groups

The home page follows your draft categories. Open Explore, switch between list and map, and try the age, cost and access filters. The list works with the map switched off.

02

Open a listing

Each listing shows its source, how it was located, when it was checked and a plain freshness band. Try a school, a trail and a beach, and tell us what is missing or wrong.

03

Ask Lumen

Use the Lumen button and ask, for example: "somewhere free for a 3 year old", "rainy day ideas", "an easy redwood walk with a pushchair". It answers only from verified records and says where each answer came from.

04

Join, gently

Join to see the welcome steps. Nothing asks about a child; every step can be skipped; settings are yours to delete.

05

Suggest and correct

Use Suggest a place, or ask Lumen to "correct: …" once signed in. Both go to a review queue; nothing publishes on its own.

06

Read the promises

About shows the moon gauge, the data licences and the governance loop. The maths behind the gauge and the freshness bands is public in the repository.
