Privacy

Privacy

Crescent Compass collects as little as it can. You can browse everything without an account, and nothing is collected about children.

Draft for Kim's approval. Effective date and contact details to be added before members can join.

Who runs the site

Crescent Compass is owned by Kim, a private individual in Crescent City, California, who decides how members' information is used. The site is built and looked after by Alex Sabine (Honami), a contractor in the United Kingdom. Alex holds the technical accounts on Kim's behalf and handles members' information only on her instructions. Kim, Daniel and Bill have full access to all of it. If you just browse

We do not ask for anything, and we do not read your location. The public map is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking. The only cookies are the ones the site needs to work: signing in, protecting forms and showing confirmation messages. If we ever add visitor counts, they will be counts only, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you, and this page will say so first.

If you join

  • Your account: a username, your email address, and your password, which is stored only in a protected, scrambled form.
  • Settings you choose, all optional: a display name, the age bands you are searching for, budget, step-free access, your community, and how much Lumen shows. These are search settings. They are not a profile of your children.
  • Places you save, and any private notes on them, visible only to you.
  • Suggestions and photos you send. Nothing is public until a steward approves it. Location data is removed from photos when they arrive.

Lumen, the guide

Lumen is automated, not a person, and can be wrong. It answers only from the published listings. It keeps no record of your conversation: each question is answered on its own, and nothing about it is stored on your device or our server. It uses your saved settings only if you allow it. Our log records what Lumen showed and why, not what you typed. When Lumen's AI language feature is switched on, your question and the published listings it might use are sent to Anthropic, which provides the AI. Anthropic's commercial terms say this data is not used to train its models by default.

Who else sees it

We never sell or rent your information, and we never share it for advertising. It is handled only by the services that run the site for Kim: the server host, the email service for sign-in and any reminders you turn on, and Anthropic for Lumen, as above. Each one uses it only to provide its service. We would share it with the authorities only if the law required it.

Children

Accounts are for adults aged 18 and over, and the site is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from or about children. If we learn that a child has an account, we delete it. Please do not upload photos that show identifiable children.

Your choices

  • Change or remove any setting at any time from My atlas.
  • Download everything we hold about you, as one file, from My atlas.
  • Delete your account from My atlas. Your settings, saved places and unreviewed suggestions go with it. Suggestions a steward has already reviewed stay, with your name removed.
  • Reminders and email summaries are off unless you turn them on, and every email has a way to stop them. We do not send text messages.
  • Browser "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals: we do no cross-site tracking, so there is nothing for them to switch off.

How long we keep it

Your account information is kept until you delete your account. Lumen keeps nothing from conversations. Our log of decisions is kept so that every change to the map can be checked, and it refers to members only by a number, never by name or email.

Keeping it safe

Sign-in and members' information stay on the site's own server, with secure connections and protected passwords, and only a few named people can reach it. If your information were ever exposed, we would tell you promptly, explain what happened, and ask you to change your password.

Questions, complaints and changes

Write to us at the contact address above and we will reply within 30 days. If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the site before the change takes effect, and show the new effective date here.
