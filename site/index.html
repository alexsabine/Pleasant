A friendly atlas for families, neighbours and newcomers.

· Crescent City · California ·

A locally governed map and quiet assistant to help people arriving in Crescent City discover places, people, services and community life, without turning anyone into a data point.

Held by the beauty of the Northern California coast, it begins with what families ask for first: parks, pools, schools, libraries, beaches, trails and the people to call.

Every listing shows its official source and the date it was last checked. Unknown is shown as unknown.

What people can do

Discover

Find schools, parks, libraries, clinics, trails, public events and local resources, each with its source and the date it was checked.

Coordinate

Ask Lumen for places, events and practical help. It answers only from verified records and shows you where every answer came from.

Belong

Save useful places, share approved information, and join community life at your own pace. Quiet is always a choice.

Core promise

The map helps people notice and care for their shared surroundings. It must never claim to be a complete picture of a place, or of the people who live there.

Lumen

I am an automated guide, not a person, and I can be wrong. I only answer from the verified directory, show where each answer came from, and say so when I don't know. Please check the listing before you go.
