E3 Summer Youth Program (DNCOE / SMART Workforce Center) Checked online

A paid summer work-experience programme for high school sophomores, juniors and seniors run through the county office of education and the SMART Workforce Center. Selected students are matched with local worksites and can earn up to 10 elective credits; applications open each spring.
